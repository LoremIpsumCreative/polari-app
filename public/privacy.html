1. What Polari Is

Polari is an educational and cultural app designed to preserve, teach and celebrate Polari, the historic queer language. The app may include dictionary entries, word collections, filters, favourite words, quizzes, learning tools, illustrations and related features.

2. Information We Collect

The information we collect depends on how you use Polari.

We may collect:

  • Account information, such as your email address, display name and authentication details;
  • App activity, such as favourite words, viewed entries, completed quizzes, quiz scores and streaks, spaced-repetition learning progress (including how well you recall a word and when it is next due for review), collections used, filters selected and app preferences;
  • Your acknowledgement of the app's content advisory, recorded against your account so you are not shown it repeatedly;
  • Suggested corrections you submit about a dictionary entry, including the entry, the field you are correcting and the text you write. You may submit these while signed out, in which case they are not linked to an account;
  • Support and feedback information, such as messages you send us, the category you choose, the app version you were using and, optionally, a contact email address you give us so we can reply. You may send feedback while signed out;
  • Technical information, such as device type, operating system, app version, browser type, IP address, language settings and performance data.

We do not intentionally collect sensitive information unless it is necessary for a specific feature, you choose to provide it, or we are required or authorised by law to collect it.

Because suggested corrections and feedback are free text, you may include personal or sensitive information in them even though we do not ask for it.

Please do not include personal or sensitive information in a suggested correction or feedback message - it is read and stored by us.

If we receive personal or sensitive information we did not solicit and could not otherwise have collected, we will delete or de-identify it as soon as practicable, unless we are required by law to keep it.

8. Local Storage and Cookies

Polari's web app stores information in your browser's local storage rather than in cookies. This is used to keep you signed in and to remember which day you last opened your word of the day.

On mobile, the equivalent information is held in your device's secure storage.

We do not use cookies or similar technologies for advertising, tracking or profiling.

You can clear this information through your browser or device settings. Some features, including staying signed in, will not work if you do.

9. Push Notifications

Polari does not currently send push notifications, and no notification service is integrated.

If we add them, we will ask for permission through your device first, you will be able to turn them off at any time in your device settings, and we will update this Privacy Policy.

10. How Suggested Corrections and Feedback are Reviewed

When you submit a suggested correction, it is stored in our database and cannot be read back by the app or by other users.

Polari's source code is kept in a public repository, and an automated daily job writes a summary of unreviewed suggestions into that repository so we are notified of them. That summary includes the dictionary entry a suggestion relates to and a count of suggestions awaiting review. It does not include the text of your suggestion, your name, email address or account identifier, or whether you were signed in when you submitted it.

The text of your suggestion is never published — it is only ever seen by us, when we review it. We still ask that you do not include personal or sensitive information in a suggested correction or feedback message, since it is read and stored by us. If you need to tell us something privately, use the Feedback form instead.

Feedback messages are stored in our database and are not published. They are readable only by us.
